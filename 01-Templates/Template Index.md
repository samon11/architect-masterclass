# Templates

Quick reference for note templates. Use `Ctrl/Cmd + P` → "Insert template" to insert.

## Available Templates

| Template | Use When |
|----------|----------|
| [[01-Templates/Course Note]] | Taking notes during a lecture/session |
| [[01-Templates/Concept Note]] | Capturing a concept, principle, or pattern |
| [[01-Templates/Book/Article Note]] | Notes from reading Juval's books or articles |

## Tips

- Every module page has topic checkboxes you can tick off as you cover them
- Use `[[double-bracket links]]` to connect related concepts across modules
- The `#juval-lowy` tag is used for all course-related notes
- The `#course/notes` tag marks lecture/lecture notes
- The `#concept` tag marks standalone concept notes
- Add `#ai`, `#design`, `#agile`, `#soa`, etc. as relevant

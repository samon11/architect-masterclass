## IDesign Approach and CMM

Process is compatible with CMM level 2-3, with some elements from level 4.

**CMM Maturity Levels (bottom to top):**

**1. Initial**

**2. Repeatable**
- ✓ Requirements Management
- ✓ Project Planning
- ✓ Project Tracking (PDMC)
- ✓ Quality Assurance
- ✓ Configuration Management
- Subcontract Management

**3. Defined**
- Organizational Process
- Integrated Management
- ✓ Software Engineering
- Inter-group Coordination
- ✓ Training Program
- ✓ Peer Reviews

**4. Managed**
- ✓ Metrics Management
- Quality Management

**5. Optimized**
- Defect Prevention
- Technology Change Management
- Process Change Management

(Checkmarks ✓ indicate items highlighted in blue; unchecked items are in black.)
